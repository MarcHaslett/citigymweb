About Citi Gym

Citi Gym is a modern facility located in Derry offering a range of health and wellness services to help members achieve their fitness Goals. We aim to provide a welcoming and supportive environment for people of all fitness levels

Our gym provides modern fitness equipment, group exercise classes and personal training services.Whether you are new to exercise or an experienced gym member, Citi Gym provides facilities and support to help you improve your health, fitness and wellbeing.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote health and fitness by providing quality facilities, qualified staff and a safe inclusive environment where members can work towards their individual fitness goals.

Our Vlaues

  • Providing a safe and welcoming fitness environment
  • Supporting members in achieving their fitness goals
  • Promoting health, fitness and wellbeing
  • Providing quality facitities and equipment
  • Offering support from qualified fitness professionals
  • Creating an inclusive environment for all fitness levels

Our Facilities

Citi Gym provides a range of modern facilities designed to support different types of training and fitness activities.
